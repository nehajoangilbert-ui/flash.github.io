Welcome to FLASH

A local authentication and session protocol designed so that one stolen factor never opens the whole account.

🔑 PasswordSlow key derivation (200,000 rounds) with a random salt. The password itself is never stored.
🧬 PasskeysA public and private key pair is made at sign-up. The private key signs a fresh challenge at every login.
📱 One-time code8 digits, valid for 3 minutes, usable once, 3 tries per code.
⏱ Short sessionsA 5-minute token with a live timer that you can revoke at any time.

  1. Register with email, phone and password, then confirm your phone.
  2. Receive your permanent public and private keys by email.
  3. Log in: password, phone OTP, signed challenge, then a final OTP.
  4. Enter a protected session that expires and can be revoked.
OTPs are sent by SMS to your phone and your passkeys by email. Enter the phone in international format, e.g. +919876543210.
